Quick wins you can do right now

No new software needed for most of these. They start plugging the gaps straight away.

1

Stop sending Meta ads straight to Zenoti

Send them to a lead form or a page like the skin assessment, so you capture a name and number from everyone who clicks.

2

Text every enquiry within 5 minutes

A personal SMS while they're still thinking about you. Speed alone lifts bookings.

3

Follow up every consultation after 48 hours

"It was lovely meeting you this week. Did you have any questions about your plan?" This is where most of the revenue is sitting.

4

Lead your ads with the consultation

"Not sure what your skin actually needs?" works better than a machine name. Let the consultation choose the treatment.

5

Get you on camera at your next content day

Short videos like "Why I wouldn't recommend this treatment for everyone". Make your content spend build your authority.

6

Ask every patient for a Google review

A text with your direct review link after each appointment.

7

Finish the website move

Redirect every old page to its new one, submit the new sitemap, and update or close the old Cleveland listing on Google.

8

Rebook before they leave

Make the next appointment part of every visit, and track rebooking for each practitioner.
