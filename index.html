The biggest gap we heard

Your Meta ads send people straight into Zenoti

Anyone who clicks, looks, is not ready, and leaves, is gone. There is no database of them, no follow-up and no second chance to convert them later. The same happens when a patient leaves a consultation with their printed plan and says "I'll think about it."

So the system sits in front of Zenoti, not instead of it:

Ad or search→Lead captured→Answered straight away→Nurtured by concern→Consultation→Zenoti→Plan follow-up

And then every lead can be traced from source to consultation to treatment revenue, so you know which campaign actually paid for itself, not just which one produced the most leads.
